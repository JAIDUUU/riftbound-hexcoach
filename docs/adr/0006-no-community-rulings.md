# 0006: Community rulings are not a runtime source

Status: accepted

## Context
Rifthunt serves per-card rulings (167 rulings on 71 cards when checked) taken from the unofficial Riftbound FAQ site under CC BY-SA 4.0. These are community-made, not Riot's official FAQs. The licence requires attribution and share-alike.

## Decision
Do not ingest community rulings as a retrieval source. They may be used as inspiration for hand-written golden-set items.

## Consequences
- No CC BY-SA attribution or share-alike obligations in the product.
- Coverage depends on the official rules, card text and Riot's FAQs only (see [0001](0001-source-precedence-faq-card-rules.md)).
- Golden-set items inspired by community rulings must be written by hand and checked against official sources.
